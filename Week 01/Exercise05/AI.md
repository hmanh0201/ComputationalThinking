## 3. Experience and Tips for Using AI Effectively and Responsibly
Based on my practical experience and the principles from the provided documents (*Responsible AI* and *CS10014 AI Usage Guidelines*), here are my core tips for using AI effectively:

### Tip 1: Apply "No Blind Copying" and Prioritize Deep Understanding
* **Experience:** AI-generated code or text can look convincing on the surface but fail on edge cases or contain logical flaws.
* **Actionable Tip:** Never copy and paste AI outputs directly into a project. Always rewrite explanations in your own words and ensure you can explain every line of code and algorithmic step during an in-class or oral examination.

### Tip 2: Cross-Verify Everything (Academic Integrity & Bias Mitigation)
* **Experience:** Because AI models are trained on vast datasets, they can suffer from data bias or "hallucinate" inaccurate references and formulas.
* **Actionable Tip:** Treat AI as a starting point, not an official academic source. Always cross-verify AI explanations against authoritative textbooks, official documentation, and empirical benchmarks.

### Tip 3: Use Structured, Step-by-Step Prompts (Explainability)
* **Experience:** Asking vague prompts like *"Solve this problem for me"* leads to poor learning outcomes and generic results.
* **Actionable Tip:** Decompose the problem first. Use structured prompts (such as Chain-of-Thought prompting) to ask AI *how* and *why* a specific method works (e.g., *"Compare the trade-offs between BFS and DFS for this constraint"*). This improves explainability and keeps you in control of the system design.

### Tip 4: Maintain Transparency and Accountability (Document Your AI Usage)
* **Experience:** As developers and students, we are 100% accountable for the code and reports we submit, regardless of whether AI assisted in the process.
* **Actionable Tip:** Keep a clear log of your AI interactions—including the tool name, version, access time, exact prompts, and how you modified or validated the output. Always declare this information honestly in the **Appendix (AI Usage Notes)** of your course reports.

### Tip 5: Respect Data Privacy and Security
* **Experience:** Responsible AI requires strict protection of personal and sensitive data.
* **Actionable Tip:** Never input private personal information, credentials, or confidential data into public AI platforms.
